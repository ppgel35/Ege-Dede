Gravitational Assist Simulation

A spaceship flies past a fixed celestial object. Choose the starting position and velocity, the object's position and strength, and the time settings, then press Run. The orbit is integrated with the leapfrog method, and you get the trajectory, speed and energy-conservation graphs. Units are dimensionless; the object's strength is the single parameter GM.
